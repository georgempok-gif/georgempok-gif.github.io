Privacy Policy — Lucky Wheels Slots

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Lucky Wheels Slots ("the app") is a free-to-play slot machine game for entertainment only. It offers no real-money gambling and no opportunity to win money or prizes. FUN coins have no cash value and cannot be sold, transferred, exchanged for money or withdrawn. Optional FUN coin packs can be bought with real money through Google Play; they are play money only and can never be cashed out.

Information we collect

The app does not ask for an account, your name, email address or other personal details. Your game progress (coin balance, bet size, VIP level, recent spin history, sound and language settings, and whether you own the ad-free upgrade) is stored only on your device.

Advertising

The free version shows banner ads served by Google AdMob, and every player can choose to watch a short rewarded video for free FUN coins. AdMob may collect and use device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID), IP address, and ad-interaction data to serve and measure ads, and, where you consent, to personalise them. Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent form. See how Google uses data from partner apps. You can reset or opt out of the advertising ID in your Android settings.

Purchases

Optional FUN coin packs and the "Ad-free" upgrade are processed by Google Play. We never receive your payment details; Google Play tells the app only that the purchase was made.

Children

The app is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed at children.
